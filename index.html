About Us

Welcome to Venus Elitee Spa

Venus Elitee Spa is a wellness and family spa in the heart of Miraj, created as a quiet retreat from busy days. From the moment you step into our lounge, the mood is unhurried — warm lighting, soft music and a welcome that puts you at ease.

Our treatment rooms are thoughtfully arranged and freshly prepared for every guest, with private spaces available for individual and couples experiences. Every session is guided by trained therapists who focus on comfort, discretion and genuine relaxation.
